# Privacy Policy — XER Logic-Guard

**Last updated: 2026**

XER Logic-Guard runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit,
store remotely, or share any personal or project data.

- **No data leaves your device.** Schedule files (XER) you load are read and
  processed locally in your browser/app. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.**
- **Local storage only.** The app may remember your typed "safe list" and
  interface codes in your browser's local storage, on your device. You can
  clear these at any time from within the app or your browser settings.
- **Your original files are never modified.** Cleaned outputs are separate copies
  you choose to download.
- **Third-party libraries.** The Excel and ZIP features use open-source libraries
  (SheetJS/xlsx-js-style and JSZip) bundled with, or loaded by, the app. They run
  locally and do not transmit your data.

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to access,
retain, or delete on your behalf.
